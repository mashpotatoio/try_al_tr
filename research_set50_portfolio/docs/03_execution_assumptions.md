# Execution assumptions and simulator gap

Completed close t creates the earliest attempted order at open t+1. The model uses the local `CommissionService` price ladder (including a THB 0.10 step at THB 10), one adverse tick per filled side, 0.157% commission and 7% VAT on commission. It limits attempted volume to 1% of trailing 20 completed session volumes and to 100-share lots; it does not use the current session's volume. Orders above the cap are recorded as partial. A separate half-fill scenario further reduces requested shares. These mechanics do not guarantee an IOC fill.

The local `tradeSim/Order.py` accepts Limit and Market, and `Execution.py` handles market orders at one adverse tick and limit orders against tick flags. Market orders have no true IOC market-to-limit depth simulation. The simulator validates cumulative opposing tick volume, cash, 100-share multiples and permitted symbols. The daily research model uses a conservative participation cap but cannot reproduce tick order, order-book depth, latency or IOC cancellation. Incoming symbols may be interleaved out of global time order; `on_data` bars can be forming, so a native adapter must wait for completed historical data and independently gate orders by session.

Daily OHLCV are vendor-revised for corporate actions; historical adjustment vintages and point-in-time constituents are missing. The one-tick ladder is defined on raw prices, so applying it to adjusted opens is only a sensitivity estimate. Missing opens skip orders. Missing later close marks carry the last observed close for existing holdings, with a limitation flag in interpretation; no future price is filled backward. The available 16 raw-tick sessions cannot form a complete 20-session native competition test. No native order run was claimed in Phase 3.
