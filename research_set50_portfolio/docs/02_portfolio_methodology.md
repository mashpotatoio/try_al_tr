# Portfolio methodology

Each window starts with THB 10 million and exactly 20 SET50 sessions. On the first morning, signals use the preceding completed close. Subsequent scheduled decisions occur every five sessions. A–D select six stocks at 80% target exposure, subject to 20% per-name cap; the rest stays cash. Equal weighting divides target exposure evenly. Inverse volatility uses inverse trailing 20-session close-return standard deviation, floors the denominator at 0.0001, then redistributes under caps. The buy-and-hold control selects six alphabetically first valid permitted symbols once; it is a reproducible simple baseline, not a random constituent basket.

E inherits the A–D configuration selected using only 11 development windows. It uses 75% target exposure and an 18% stock cap; a separate `risk_only` control applies these two changes without turnover controls. E also retains holdings until rank 10 and skips trades smaller than 3% of estimated equity. A–D all use the same scheduled exit rule and execution assumptions. Alternative rank and trend-deterioration exit scenarios are separate exploratory analyses.

At each rebalance, target shares are estimated from previous close and rounded down to 100 shares. Sells execute before buys. Target weights, requested shares, accepted shares after cash and participation checks, modeled filled shares, holdings, and close-mark equity are recorded separately. End-window holdings are marked to market and are not artificially liquidated for round-trip counts.

The benchmark starts at the first session open, matching the first possible portfolio entry, then marks at each close through session 20. Maximum drawdown uses initial capital or initial benchmark open as the first peak. Exposure is the mean of 20 end-of-session invested-value/NAV fractions. Expected payoff is mean net P&L of nonbreakeven completed original FIFO lots in THB. A partially sold original lot counts only when fully closed, matching local engine behavior. Competition Calmar is cumulative net return divided by absolute maximum drawdown, undefined at zero drawdown.
