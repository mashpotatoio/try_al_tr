# Phase 1 comparison

Phase 1's saved native table reports 16 sessions from 2026-09-09 through 2026-09-30. Momentum returned −6.5544% versus −4.1544% for SET50, with 95.45% average exposure, 16 FIFO round-trips, negative THB 24,999.71 expected payoff and 3.5147 turnover. The benchmark-relative equal-weight control has exactly the same outcome. Relative strength with inverse-volatility sizing returned −6.8110%, with 3.9240 turnover and negative THB 28,801.15 expected payoff. This is direct evidence that changing sizing worsened this particular comparison; it does not show that inverse-volatility sizing is generally harmful.

RSI oversold returned −2.9185%, or +1.2358 percentage points against the benchmark, but average exposure was only 34.20% and expected payoff was negative THB 21,897.39. Low market participation is a plausible contributor to its smaller loss in a declining index period. The result cannot establish a positive stock-selection effect. Across Phase 1's 24 daily evaluation windows, RSI had negative mean excess return and beat the index in only 5 windows.

The Phase 2 date-level IC, quantile, and cost outputs should be used to test six explanations without selecting a favorable subperiod: (1) momentum rankings lacked predictive power; (2) they had some ranking power but gross magnitude was inadequate; (3) turnover and costs overwhelmed it; (4) sizing altered risk and cash allocation independently of rankings; (5) oversold positions benefited from cash during index losses; and (6) regime or horizon dependence matters. Momentum and benchmark-relative momentum are algebraically the same ranking on each date. Their ICs should match except for missing-data differences, and no independent relative-strength alpha can be claimed from the subtraction alone.

The native period is too short for a full 20-session competition run. A 20-session forward label formed after a signal exits 21 sessions later and generally cannot be completed within a 20-session competition window.
