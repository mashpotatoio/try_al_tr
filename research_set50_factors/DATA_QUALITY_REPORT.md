# Data quality and timeline

The source audit reads the 48 names in `marketInfo/symbolSET50/Symbol_SET50.csv` and 49 daily OHLCV files, including SET50. The common SET50 calendar has 738 sessions from 2023-09-21 to 2026-10-06. Histories differ: BANPU has 45 rows, GULF 363, MRDIYT 223, THAI 286, and TIDLOR 339; other names have 738 in the Phase 1 audit. There are no missing sessions within each symbol's observed span against the supplied calendar, but that calendar is not independently certified. The new run's `data_inventory.csv` records every symbol's exact coverage, invalid rows, zero-volume count, and file hash.

Only 16 sessions of raw ticks are supplied, 2026-09-09 through 2026-09-30. Phase 1 counted 2,906,167 tick rows, including 1,046,005 exact repeats. No trade identifiers justify removing repeated prints. Intraday feature files exist at nine intervals but are not needed for the daily hypotheses. The scoring benchmark CSV overlaps the daily SET50 close series on the native dates; the historical daily SET50 file supplies the longer research calendar. A SET50 row on 2025-12-11 has `Open > High`; any label requiring that open is invalidated, while its valid close can be used in close-only benchmark features.

The supplied history guide says historical prices are revised for capital actions. It provides no action ledger, dividend cash flows, adjustment factors, or as-published vintages. Daily adjusted **opens** and **closes** are used consistently for historical labels and features, without claiming executable fill prices. The package never applies the exchange tick ladder to adjusted prices. The `raw_tick_cost_reference.csv` uses observed raw first-tick prices solely as a 16-session one-tick cost reference, without claiming those ticks were fillable for an order of the desired size.

Historical membership snapshots are absent. Today's 48-name permitted list is therefore used for every historical date, with explicit survivorship bias. Missing listings, invalid OHLC, zero volume, or missing opens are not backfilled. A forward label requires the complete sequence of `t+1` through `t+1+h` valid opens on the common calendar. The run's `timeline.csv`, date-level IC table, and quantile table document cutoff and forward dates; all times are session labels in Asia/Bangkok.

The Phase 1 full-file audit and anomaly discussion remain in `research_set50_baseline/DATA_QUALITY_REPORT.md`. This Phase 2 report should be read with each run's `data_inventory.csv` and `observation_status.csv`, which show actual exclusions by factor and horizon.
