# Execution and data validation

The frozen Phase 3 run reproduced 356/356 rows exactly: maximum absolute difference across the 14 checked numerical and eligibility fields is 0, tolerance 1e-10. This validates deterministic reproduction, not economic realism.

Raw tick coverage is 16 sessions (2026-09-09 through 2026-09-30). Across 768 overlapping stock-days, 768 have identical daily and tick OHLC under auction-aware reconstruction. The 16 tick files are **not globally timestamp sorted**; within-symbol timestamps were sorted before reconciliation. This confirms no visible adjustment difference in this short overlap. It does not establish historical adjustment factors for 2023–2026 or validate a one-tick fill on adjusted daily prices. Earlier corporate-action ratios require raw historical prices or vendor adjustment metadata. The tick reconstruction uses OPEN1_E and ATC prints where present; absence is flagged in the CSV. A matching OHLC does not reveal executable bid/ask depth.

Local tick ladder equals `tradeSim.CommissionService`: steps are THB 0.01 below 2, .02 from 2, .05 from 5, .10 from 10, .25 from 25, .50 from 100, 1 from 200, and 2 from 400. At boundary prices the higher step applies. Applying that ladder to adjusted historical prices may produce non-executable levels when adjustment factors differ from one.

The isolated native probe on 2026-09-09 submitted a 100-share ADVANC Market buy at raw last price 353.0. Cash debit was THB 35,459.46846, differing from the independently computed one-tick plus fee/VAT debit by THB -8.15e-10. This checks a single native fill and cash entry. A separate frozen-A signal replay across all 16 tick sessions made 30 native order attempts and 29 fills. Its ending NAV was THB 9,472,514.72 versus THB 9,511,308.44 for a matched 16-session daily-model replay, a THB -38,793.72 difference. Native completed 9 original FIFO lots versus 10 in the daily model. The native adapter waits for observed opposing cumulative tick volume and orders at the first eligible chronological tick, while the daily model assumes capped next open fills; therefore both price and holdings paths differ. The replay is a practice-engine diagnostic, not an official IOC backtest or a 20-session competition outcome. Native `Order.py` accepts Limit and Market; `Execution.py` models immediate one-tick market fills and resting tick-flag-based limit fills. No native Market-to-Limit IOC depth model or official validator was found.

Reconciliation: `daily_tick_reconciliation.csv`, `tick_session_alignment.csv`, `inferred_adjustment_ratios.csv`, `native_probe.json`. Differences are not automatically labeled corporate actions; this overlap shows none. Future earlier-date discrepancies must be classified with vendor metadata before changing the backtester.
